# Community and group ontology

`Place` is geographic; `Community` is a population/identity; `Group` is an interaction space; `Membership` is a scoped relation; `EconomicOrganization` is an independent accountable entity. Never merge these.

## Faceted group taxonomy
- **Geography:** village, village cluster, growth point, ward, neighbourhood, town, district, province, national, cross-border.
- **Purpose:** general community, work, occupational trade, agriculture, products, procurement, cooperative, logistics, diaspora.
- **Membership:** open, request-to-join, invite-only, restricted or business-managed.
- **Governance:** member-moderated, assigned moderators, cooperative-managed, business-managed or platform-managed.

A person may belong to multiple groups and a group may cover several places. One canonical offer may be shared to many groups, without duplicating stock ownership or exposing restricted discussions. Membership is not verified residence, qualification or identity. Group admins cannot grant credential verification or independently control members' funds.

## Economic networks
Village producers → participating collection hub → independent transport → urban/remote/diaspora buyers. Community agents can assist with consent, but never own the user's account or proceeds merely by assisting.
