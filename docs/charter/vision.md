# Product vision and scope

**Rese Rese:** Zimbabwe-first AI-native grassroots economic network, built from villages and growth points to cities and cross-border demand.

## One core
People and organizations offer skills, services, labour, goods, rental, transport and legitimate business capabilities. Buyers and communities publish work requests, procure goods, engage providers and record authorized transactions. **Skills, Communities and GEO are experiences on one canonical graph.**

## Inclusion
Every recognized settlement should be represented or provisionally discoverable even without a formal postcode, known coordinate, paved road or smartphone. Support Shona, Zimbabwean Ndebele, English and other languages as quality permits; voice, text, low-data web, offline drafts and authorized community agents.

## Geography and sectors
Cities, suburbs, villages, farming areas, growth points, remote mountains, mining-adjacent settlements, water crossings and economic corridors. Access to restricted mines/underground facilities requires legal permissions, physical verification and safety constraints; never invent live underground positioning.

## Economic flows
Multi-skill providers, service directories, job postings, goods/produce, cooperative aggregation, collection hubs, driver logistics, diaspora buyers and independent trust checks. Individual stock and payment ownership survives any group aggregation.

## Boundaries
Free API/open-source-first; Rust + TrailBase canonical foundation; no separate Tengasell integration without authorization; no surveillance-by-default; no unverified credentials, locations, prices or delivery claims. See [full master plan](../source/RR-MASTER-01.original.md).
