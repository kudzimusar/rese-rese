# ADR-0001 — Unified economic core
**Status:** Accepted conceptual direction · **Date:** 2026-10-28

## Decision
Build a Zimbabwe-first, AI-native, place-aware marketplace with three experiences: Rese Rese Skills, Rese Rese Communities, and Rese Rese GEO. They share canonical people/organizations, occupations, skills, services, goods, places, groups, offers, work requests, quotes, transport, evidence and transactions.

## Consequences
Do not create independent customer, place, provider or ownership records in separate products. The knowledge graph is a relational/semantic relationship model, not a mandate to purchase a graph database. All workflows preserve ownership, permissions and temporal history. This ADR governs every future subsystem.
